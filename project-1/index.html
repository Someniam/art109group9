Public Domain Day 2026

Here is an archive of a selection of creative works that entered the public domain in the year 2026. Most were published in 1930, as the United States’ copyright term defined by law lasts for 95 years after first publication, expiring on the first of January of the following year. We were inspired by Creative Commons

Below is a table featuring a list of works, the artist(s) that made them and a link to access them on the Internet Archive or on Wikimedia Commons. Also is an indication whether or not the work is also public domain in countries with a life + 50 or life + 70 year copyright term. If not, then the date it will enter the public domain is listed.

Note: While the copyrights on these works may have expired, one must be cautious around trademarks, which are active as long as they are renewed. Trademarks are used to recognize a brand and to avoid consumer confusion. This means that you can’t claim that your product was sponsored by the company or estate that created the work, which can include using the original title of the work at all to advertise a derivative work based on the now public domain incarnation. You can, however, use trademarked names of characters inside of a creative work as long as you don’t advertise it as mentioned before.
